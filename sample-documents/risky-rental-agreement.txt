RENTAL AGREEMENT
This agreement is between Priya Sharma (Landlord) and Arjun Kumar (Tenant) dated 12th March 2025.
1. TERM. The tenancy is for 36 months and shall automatically renew for another year unless the Landlord agrees otherwise.
2. RENT. Tenant shall pay INR 45,000 per month. Late payment shall incur interest at 36% per annum.
3. TERMINATION. The Landlord may terminate this agreement at its sole discretion with 7 days notice.
4. DEPOSIT. The security deposit is non-refundable and may be forfeited by the Landlord.
5. LIABILITY. Tenant accepts unlimited liability for all claims related to the premises.
6. JURISDICTION. Courts of Hyderabad shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
